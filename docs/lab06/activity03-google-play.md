# Activity 03 – Google Play Console

**Status: NOT DONE. Requires a real Google Play Console account.**

## Steps (to be done by the student)
1. Create a Google Play developer account (one-time registration fee applies).
2. Create app → name FitFlow, language, App / Free.
3. Complete App content: privacy policy URL, ads, target audience, data safety form.
4. Upload the signed `.aab` to Internal testing (Testing → Internal testing).
5. Add testers and share the opt-in link.
6. Add store listing text and screenshots (see Activity 02).
